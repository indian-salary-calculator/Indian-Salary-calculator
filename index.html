Frequently Asked Questions

What is in-hand salary?

It is the amount left after applicable payroll deductions from your salary.

Is CTC equal to take-home salary?

No. CTC can include employer contributions and benefits that are not paid as monthly cash.

Is this calculator exact?

No. It is an estimate. Use your payslip and official tax information for final payroll calculations.
